# Trash Qwins — Official Website

> **Turning Waste into Wealth.**  
> Waste Recovery | Resource Innovation | Sustainable Livelihoods

Trash Qwins is a Kenyan waste-to-value company transforming discarded glass bottles and organic waste into valuable resources through recovery, aggregation, recycling, and Black Soldier Fly (BSF) technology.

---

## 🌿 Brand Identity
* **Primary Brand Color**: Dark Green (`#174D32`) — *Nature • Trust • Sustainability*
* **Accent Brand Color**: Mustard Yellow (`#B58A16`) — *Energy • Creativity • Value*
* **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans & Outfit
