Company Profile

Pioneering Practical Circular Value Chains Across Kenya

Trash Qwins (often searched as TrashQwins) is a Kenyan waste-to-value company focused on transforming waste into valuable resources through recovery, aggregation, recycling, and value addition.

We initially concentrate on two high-impact, underutilised streams: discarded glass bottles (Qwins Glass) and organic waste bioconversion using Black Soldier Fly (BSF) technology (Qwins Organics), reinforced by aggregation hubs and advisory services (Qwins Solutions).

Working alongside hotels, restaurants, waste pickers, Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs), recyclers, agribusinesses, and county governments, we prove that waste is not merely a disposal burden—it is a recoverable economic resource.
