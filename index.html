1. About Trash Qwins

About Trash Qwins — Pioneering Kenya's Circular Economy

Trash Qwins is a Kenyan waste-to-value company focused on transforming waste into valuable resources through recovery, aggregation, recycling, and value addition.

The company is initially concentrating on glass bottles and organic waste, using glass recovery and Black Soldier Fly (BSF) technology to unlock the economic potential of materials that are often discarded.

We work with communities, waste pickers, hotels, restaurants, institutions, Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs), recyclers, and agribusinesses to build practical circular-economy value chains. Our approach combines commercial thinking with environmental responsibility: waste is not simply something to dispose of, but a resource that can be recovered, transformed, and returned to productive use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything About Trash Qwins (TrashQwins)

Answers to common questions about our Kenyan circular economy operations, glass upcycling, and Black Soldier Fly (BSF) solutions.

What is Trash Qwins (TrashQwins)?

Trash Qwins—often searched as one word, TrashQwins—is a Kenyan waste-to-value company headquartered in Nairobi. We transform discarded glass bottles and organic food waste into commercial resources, green jobs, and sustainable livelihoods through collection, aggregation, artisan upcycling, and Black Soldier Fly (BSF) technology.

What services and products does Trash Qwins offer?

Trash Qwins operates through three integrated pillars: Qwins Glass (hospitality bottle collection, sorted glass cullet supply, and handcrafted upcycled tumblers, vases, and painted decor), Qwins Organics (Black Soldier Fly larvae protein feed for poultry, fish, and pigs, plus organic soil frass fertilizer), and Qwins Solutions (waste aggregation hubs, Material Recovery Facility coordination, circular economy consultancy, and training).

Where does Trash Qwins operate and who do you partner with?

Based in Nairobi, Kenya, Trash Qwins partners with hotels, restaurants, bars, Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs), waste picker alliances, community-based organizations (CBOs), glass recyclers, agribusinesses, livestock farmers, and county governments across Kenya.

How does Qwins Organics convert food waste with Black Soldier Fly (BSF) technology?

Suitable food scraps, market biomass, and agro-processing residues are separated at source and fed to Black Soldier Fly larvae in controlled bioconversion operations. This diverts methane-producing waste from landfills while harvesting nutrient-dense insect protein feed and mineral-rich organic frass fertilizer for Kenyan agriculture.

What upcycled glassware products does Qwins Glass make?

Beyond bulk bottle recovery and cullet supply for glass recyclers, Trash Qwins upcycles salvaged wine and spirit bottles into smoothly cut and polished drinking tumblers, botanical vases, custom candleholders, and hand-painted floral decorative bottles for hospitality venues, events, and home interiors.

How can I contact or partner with TrashQwins?
